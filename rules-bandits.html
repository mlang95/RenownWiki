Bandits

Outlaw Country

Outlaw Country — Uncontrollable Territories where Bandit Camps spawn, one camp per Territory. A Territory is in your Outlaw Country while it is within your Province. Destroying the Bandit Camp on it doesn't remove it.

Outlaw Country is counted by Territory, and each Territory holds at most one Bandit Camp. Each Province starts the game with 3 Outlaw Country Territories.

  • Your Outlaw Country (Deploy): demarcate 3 Territories in your starting region, each within Range 1 of at least one other Outlaw Country Territory and at least Range 2 from any Settlement — the first exactly Range 2 from your Town, and none within Range 2 of your Hamlet. If that's not possible, place them Range 2 from your capital Settlement.

An Outlaw Country Territory in no player's Province belongs to no one: its camp grows, but takes no action until it's within a player's Province.

Outlaw Country stays. Destroying the Bandit Camp on an Outlaw Country Territory leaves the Territory open: it's still Outlaw Country, and a new camp can be placed on it.

New Outlaw Country. A player who places a new Outlaw Country Territory (see Spawning Bandit Camps) places it at least Range 2 from every Settlement, either adjacent to their Outlaw Country or exactly Range 2 from their capital Settlement.

Spawning Bandit Camps

Every new Bandit Camp has 10 Bandits (Retinues) and an empty Treasury. An open Territory is an Outlaw Country Territory with no Bandit Camp on it.

  • Setup: at the end of Setup, each player places 1 Bandit Camp on an open Territory of their Outlaw Country, and each Wild zone gains 1. Bandit Camps don't spawn in Spring.
  • Uprising: each of its camps goes on an open Territory of your Outlaw Country; if none is open, place a new Outlaw Country Territory and the camp on it.
  • Foster Rebellion: places a new Outlaw Country Territory and its camp together (see Foster Rebellion).

Growing Bandit Camps

A Bandit Camp grows each Empire Phase by the Era's Retinues per turn, up to 25 Retinues; it never sends out a Bandit Army. Its Retinues carry the Era's armament.

EraRetinues Gained per TurnArmament
Founding1Levies: Farm Tool + Cloth
Ascension2Levies: Spear + Gambeson
Eminence3Levies: Arming Sword + Kite Shield + Leather
Zenith4Warriors: Bastard Sword + Chainmail

Bandit Rules

Bandits share the same Renown level as players. Every Bandit Camp and Army has the Era's Standing in every Domain: Untested in Founding, Rising in Ascension, Established in Eminence, Sovereign in Zenith.

All players are At War with all Bandits and their camps. Players abstain on all Bandit actions, but innate modifiers can still apply and cause the action to Fail.

While there are 10+ Bandits (Retinues) in a camp, roll a d10 each turn:

d10Cunning Action
1–4Raze
5–6Intercept Caravan
7–8Sabotage
9–10Destabilize

A camp's action targets the Settlements and Armies of every player whose Province covers the camp, the nearest first. If the camp is in no player's Province, it takes no action.

A Bandit Camp keeps Extorted money in its Treasury and doesn't pay costs, Supply, Upkeep, or recoup. When you destroy a Bandit Camp, you gain its Spoils of War from the bank (see Attacking a Bandit Camp) and Extort its whole Treasury. A destroyed camp's Destabilize or Intercept Caravan that hasn't resolved yet ends: you keep that gold.

Bandit Army Behavior

Bandit Armies are a Bandit Option (see Bandit Options).

After Bandit Mechanics resolve, a Bandit Army performs a Move action based on, in order:

  • Battle — if another player's Army is in range.
  • Pillage — if a player's Settlement is in range by a direct route (one that Presence doesn't make longer): end adjacent to the one with the highest Holding income. Each Summer, in the Extort step, the Bandit Army Extorts that Settlement's Holding income into its camp's Treasury, until it fights a Battle or leaves. A Settlement can have only one Bandit Army Pillaging it.
  • March — toward the player's Settlement with the highest Holding income, along a direct route; where Presence blocks that route, stop short of it rather than going around.

Note: if targets tie, the Host chooses the target.

Hardcore

The Host may make the game Hardcore at the Wild Outlaw Country Setup step. In a Hardcore game:

  • Wild zones: the Host places Wild zones on the parts of the map outside every player's Province, at most 1, each at least Range 1 from every player's Province and at least Range 4 from every other Outlaw Country zone.
  • Armament: Bandits are armed by Era from this table instead:
EraArmament
FoundingLevies: Pitchfork + Gambeson
AscensionWarriors: Spear + Targe Shield + Leather
EminenceWarriors: Halberd + Chainmail
ZenithMen-at-Arms: Bastard Sword + Tower Shield + Full Plate
  • Bandit Army Behavior: with Bandit Armies on, a Bandit Army targets the player's Settlement with the highest Holding income (if several tie, the closest), and performs its Move action based on, in order:
  • Pillage — if that Settlement is in range: end adjacent to it and Pillage it as above.
  • Battle — otherwise, if another player's Army is in range: the closest.
  • Lay Siege — otherwise, if a player's Settlement is in range: the closest.
  • March — otherwise, toward that Settlement, along a direct route; where Presence blocks that route, stop short of it rather than going around.

Note: if targets tie, the Host chooses the target.

Bandit Options

At the Wild Outlaw Country Setup step the Host may change any of these from the standard rules:

  • Outlaw Country doesn't grow: no new Outlaw Country Territory is ever placed. With no open Territory, a Spring or Uprising camp isn't placed; Foster Rebellion places its camp on an open Territory of the target's Outlaw Country, if any.
  • Outlaw Country clears: destroying a Bandit Camp clears its Territory; it's no longer Outlaw Country.
  • Bandit Standing by size: a Bandit Camp or Army has +2 Cunning and +2 Prowess for every 5 Retinues in it, instead of the Era's Standing.
  • Spring camps: no camps are placed at Setup. Instead, in the Empire Phase each Spring, each player places one Bandit Camp on an open Territory of their Outlaw Country (if none is open, a new Outlaw Country Territory instead; its camp comes the next Spring), and each Wild zone gains one.
  • Bandit Mechanics in Spring: Spring runs the full Bandit Mechanics — Bandit actions, Uprisings and Bandit Army Moves — as in any other Season.
  • Bandit Armies: a Bandit Camp at 25 Retinues sends out a Bandit Army of 25, and the camp stays on its Territory. While its Army is out, the camp doesn't grow or send out another Army, can't be attacked, and no camp can spawn on its Territory. After each Battle it doesn't lose, the Army returns and the camp re-musters toward 25; a Bandit Army that loses a Battle is destroyed, and its camp re-musters by growth. Destroying a Bandit Army gains its Spoils of War and Extorts its own Treasury. Bandit Armies Move by Bandit Army Behavior.

Attacking a Bandit Camp

Use the Battle action to end adjacent to a Bandit Camp, then resolve it as a Battle in the Battle Phase. Another player rolls the Bandits' Tactic each Skirmish and makes their to-Strike and Save rolls:

d10Tactic
1Scout
2Ambush
3Flank
4–6Charge
7–8Fighting Formation
9–10Defensive Formation

Winning the Battle destroys the Bandit Camp, whether its Bandits are wiped out or Rout: gain Spoils of War from the bank equal to its Retinue type's Spoils of War value (the Era's armament; see Mustering), then Extort its whole Treasury. Bandits never Fall Back, but they may Rout as normal.
