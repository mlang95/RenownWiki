Rules · Your Empire15 / 39

Movement

Speed X — An Army's movement in Territories per Move action. Base Speed is 5.

Armies move with the Move action (see Prowess Actions).

Rule: Commanding Presence

Presence — Territory within Range 1 of a non-allied Army (Bandit Armies included, Bandit Camps not), or of a Settlement while its owner has a Garrison. Presence +N raises the Range of its owner's Settlement Presence by N.

An Army can't move within a non-allied Presence unless that Move performs a Battle against it. A Move that performs a Battle or Lay Siege may pass through the Presence of its target only, and can't end within any other non-allied Presence — an Army within the Presence of two non-allied pieces can attack neither. An Army that begins a Move within a non-allied Presence may only move to end outside every Presence (or, if at War, to Battle or Lay Siege); if it can't, it can't move.
