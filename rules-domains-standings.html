Domains & Standings

Domain Standings

Domains are the identity of your character in Renown. As you gain Renown each turn, you’ll gain access to a Domain point, which you can spend in any of the four Domains. Every time you spend a Domain point, you increase your Domain value by 1 in that Domain. When your Domain value becomes three, you become Rising in that Domain, allowing you spend an additional influence on another player’s envoy of that domain, and your envoys gain influence +1. This continues when you have a domain value of six, causing you to become Established in that domain, and Sovereign when your reach a domain value of ten.

Each standing you gain in a domain makes you harder to oppose in that domain politically and allows you oppose others more easily with your influence. In addition, you gain a unique effect at each standing that makes it easier to achieve the objectives of that domain.
