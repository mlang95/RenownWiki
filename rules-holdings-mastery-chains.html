Holdings & Mastery Chains

Holding — A building you Build into a Settlement Ward, or into its Mastery Chain parent's Ward. Its effect applies while it's active. The full list is in the Holdings chapter.

Mastery Chain — A Holding's Mastery Chain is the line of Holdings it builds from, usually running from a Raw Material up to a Monument.

  • You may start building a Holding only while you control one complete line of its Mastery Chain, anywhere in your empire; this is checked only when you start it.
  • A Root Holding has no Mastery Chain requirement.
  • A Holding placed in the same Settlement Ward as its immediate parent shares that Ward, and the chain can continue upward the same way. Each Holding carries at most one descendant in its Ward.
  • You can't build a Holding onto one whose Build Timer is still active. A Damaged Holding under a Repair Timer can still be built onto.

Scope

Each Holding and Infrastructure has a Scope that limits how many of it can exist.

  • Settlement — one per Settlement (Raw Materials).
  • Empire — one per player (every other Holding and Infrastructure).
  • Realm — one per game (Monuments and Wonders). A Monument is taken once any player has placed it, including while it's under construction. A Wonder is taken only once it's built (see Infrastructure).
Glossary · Settlements & Holdings
TermDefinition
SettlementA Village, Town, City or Metropolis (or your Hamlet). See 8.1 (Settlements).
HamletYour farmland Settlement, placed at setup: no Tax Income or Muster Limit, 3 Wards for Natural Holdings only. See 8.1.5 (Hamlets).
Settlement WardA slot in a Settlement that holds one Holding; a Settlement has one Ward per tier (a Hamlet has 3 Wards, Natural Holdings only). See 8.1.2 (Settlement Wards).
HoldingA building you Build into a Settlement Ward, or into its Mastery Chain parent's Ward. Its effect applies while it's active.
RootA Holding with no Mastery Chain requirement: you may start it without controlling a parent. See 8.2.4 (Holdings & Mastery Chains).
NaturalA Holding property: it pays no Upkeep and may be built in a Hamlet.
MonumentA Domain's capstone Holding, one per game. Having one completes the Monument Edict.
Mastery ChainA Holding's Mastery Chain is the line of Holdings it builds from, usually running from a Raw Material up to a Monument. See 8.2.2 (Holdings & Mastery Chains).
Mastery Chain XOn a Holding tile: placed in the same Settlement Ward as X (the Raw Material or Holding named on it), it shares that Ward instead of taking its own. See 8.2.2 (Holdings & Mastery Chains).
InfrastructureAn empire-wide building that applies to every Settlement you have. 5 tiers, Primitive to Wondrous; each needs one active Infrastructure of the tier below. See 8.3 (Infrastructure).
WonderWondrous-tier Infrastructure, one per game. See 8.3 (Infrastructure).
DamagedA Holding or Infrastructure hit by Raze, Sack or Bandits: it has no effect and pays no Upkeep until Repaired.
